Small program:

CREATE compare_power_and_lcm FROM x AND y TOO
GENESIS
    CONVICTED x STATS y MOG x DIFF y GUILTY
        STEAL "Power sum is larger"
    INNOCENT
        STEAL "LCM is larger or equal"
    APOCALYPSE
APOCALYPSE

RECRUIT m AS NUMBER
RECRUIT n AS NUMBER
SHOVE m
SHOVE n
